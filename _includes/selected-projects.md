### Learning When to Intervene in Software Agents

Developing a framework for learning when an autonomous software agent should continue its current trajectory versus reassess its strategy, using verified downstream execution outcomes rather than subjective judgments of intermediate actions.

Use matched counterfactual continuations and measure complete held-out task success under fixed budgets.

**Engineering:** Built infrastructure for trajectory checkpointing, state restoration, execution-identity validation, authoritative outcome parsing, budget accounting, holdout protection, and reproducible policy evaluation.

**Research principle:** Evaluation is based on authoritative software execution outcomes rather than whether an intermediate action merely appears reasonable.

### Benchmark Reliability at Scale

Audited a 109-file benchmark environment containing approximately 540k tokens, machine-checking 100 cited evidence passages, validating page/row/line citations, independently re-deriving quantitative results, and reproducing ground truth for 8 of 9 evaluated questions.

Focused on semantic failures that survive schema and formatting checks: source-of-truth contradictions, inconsistent rubrics and test suites, and scoring defects capable of silently corrupting evaluation or training signals.

</div>
